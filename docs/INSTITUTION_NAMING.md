# Institution naming decision

The public cohort name is:

> The First Affiliated Hospital of University of South China (USC-FAH)

Ethics approval `2025LL0115001` is reported for this institution.

Official University of South China materials list **The First Affiliated Hospital of University of South China** and **Affiliated Nanhua Hospital, University of South China** as separate institutions. The public manuscript, figures, tables, and documentation therefore do not use “Nanhua Hospital” or “NH” as the legal name for the USC-FAH cohort.

Some protected local filenames and dictionary keys retain `NH` solely for backward compatibility. Those internal identifiers do not define the public legal institution name.
